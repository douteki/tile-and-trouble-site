# Tile & Trouble — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: September 2026*

Tile & Trouble is a Discord bot that runs a word-tile game. This page explains what it stores and why.

## What we store

When you use the bot, it stores the minimum needed to run a game:

- **Your Discord user ID** and **display name** at the time you joined a game, so the bot knows whose turn it is and can show scores.
- **Game state**: the board, each player's tiles, scores, move history, and the ID of the Discord thread and server the game is in.
- **Results** of finished games (player IDs, names, scores, wins) for the per-server leaderboard.

That's all. The bot does not read messages in your server (it has no message-content access), does not store message text, and does not collect email addresses, IP addresses, or anything from outside Discord.

## What we don't do

- We don't sell or share this data with anyone.
- We don't use it for advertising or analytics.
- We don't track you across servers beyond the leaderboard for each individual server.

## Where it lives and for how long

Data is stored in a database on the hosting provider the bot runs on. Game state is kept while a game is active and after it finishes so the thread's history makes sense. Practice games aren't recorded on any leaderboard.

## Removing your data

Leave a game with `/resign`. To have your user ID and results removed entirely, send a request with `/feedback` in Discord and it will be deleted from the database.

## Children

The bot is intended for users who meet Discord's minimum age requirements and is not directed at children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated. Continued use of the bot after a change means you accept the updated policy.

## Contact

Use the `/feedback` command in Discord — it goes straight to the person who runs the bot. `/about` in Discord also shows any support links.
